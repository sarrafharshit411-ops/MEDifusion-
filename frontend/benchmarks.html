Evaluation Protocol & Governance Note

All models undergo strict stratified train/validation/test partitioning (typically 75% train, 15% validation, 10% held-out test). Test cohorts are strictly unobserved during hyperparameter search, learning rate scheduling, and early stopping. Multi-class ROC curves and area under curves (AUC) are evaluated using one-vs-rest (OvR) macro-averaging.

CLINICAL GOVERNANCE NOTICE: MediFusion AI is an educational and research decision-support prototype. Empirical performance metrics reported on this page reflect laboratory benchmark datasets and must not be used as standalone clinical diagnostic determinations without professional physician oversight.
